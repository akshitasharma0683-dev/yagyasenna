Everyday Safety Tips

01

Before travelling

Share your plans with someone you trust and keep your phone charged.

02

While travelling

Stay aware of your surroundings and use trusted transport options.

03

Online safety

Protect personal information, use strong passwords and save evidence of online harassment.

04

When you feel unsafe

Move towards a safer public place and contact emergency services or someone you trust.
